Detailed Description

Loads a glTF/glb 3D model into CPU-side Model data.

Sole owner of the vendored cgltf library (system-primitive-style confinement, like Texture for stb_image): no other class calls a cgltf function. It parses the file, loads its buffers and decodes geometry, materials and the scene graph. It does not touch OpenGL and does not render — GPU upload and drawing belong to other classes.
