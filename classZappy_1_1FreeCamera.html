Detailed Description

Free-fly perspective camera: move with the keyboard, look with the mouse.

The camera is a position plus a yaw/pitch heading. Mouse drag turns the view, keyboard input moves it along its own axes (forward/strafe) and the world up. Used as the 3D-mode camera when free-fly is selected, alongside the orbit camera.
