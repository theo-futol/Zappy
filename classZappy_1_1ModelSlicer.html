Detailed Description

Extracts a single sub-object out of a multi-object Model, keyed by texture.

A decorative scene (e.g. a pile of crystals and rocks) packs many distinct objects into one Model. This slicer rebuilds a standalone Model holding only the primitives whose texture matches a given name, with their geometry baked into world space so the result is self-contained (a single identity root node) and can be normalized and placed on its own, like any other model.
