Detailed Description

One drawable chunk of a model: CPU-side geometry plus its texture reference.

Attributes are stored de-interleaved (one vector per channel) as glTF delivers them; the GPU-upload layer is free to interleave them. normals/texcoords are empty when the source primitive lacks that attribute.
