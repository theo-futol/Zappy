Detailed Description

Full-screen configuration menu: host/port text fields and a 2D/3D toggle.

Rendered into the shared window/context owned by Core. It runs its own blocking loop until the user validates (returns the chosen settings) or quits. The menu borrows the window and graphics context; it owns its own small asset cache and text renderer, loaded once and reused across visits.
