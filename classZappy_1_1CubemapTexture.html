Detailed Description

A GPU cube-map texture built from a single 4x3 horizontal-cross image.

The source image packs the six faces in the classic cross layout (columns x rows of equal square tiles): +Y on top, the -X/+Z/+X/-Z band in the middle, and -Y at the bottom. The six tiles are cut out and uploaded to the matching cube-map faces, ready to be sampled by a skybox shader.
