Detailed Description

One team in the game: its name, its connection slots, and its eggs.

A free connection slot is the right to connect a new AI player. Slots come from the initial count plus every egg laid (each egg adds a slot), so the egg list and the slot counters are kept consistent by addEgg()/removeEgg().
