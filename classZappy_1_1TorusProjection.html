Detailed Description

3D-mode projection wrapping the (toroidal) Zappy grid onto a real 3D torus.

The Zappy map already wraps around in both axes, so it is topologically a torus. This projection maps each cell to a point on the inner wall of a torus tube: grid X turns around the main ring, grid Y turns around the tube section. The surface normal points inward (toward the tube centerline), so an entity placed here stands on the inner wall with its head toward the tube's center — on the top of the torus the head naturally points down.
